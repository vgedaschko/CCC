II · Management implications

Big D or little d?

Three moves for managers.

1 Dd

Decide how to decide — and label it D or d.

[for X Y: which protocol question would we answer differently?]

  • In the team charter: vote or boss? 100 percent agreement? A deadline? Flexibility after it? p. 160
  • Label each decision: “a small d or a big D?” pp. 160–161
2

Don’t read Deciding off Leading.

[for X Y: …]

  • Titles and formality don’t show who decides: US, Germany, Japan, Vietnam. pp. 145–146, 154 · Country Mapping
  • Merger integration, onboarding, expatriates: check both scales.
3

Time your influence. “Decisive” is culturally coded.

[for X Y: …]

  • Consensual: get in early and informally (nemawashi). pp. 157–158
  • Top-down: the boss decides; follow, stay flexible. pp. 159–160
