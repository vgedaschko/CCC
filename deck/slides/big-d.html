Explain the difference between consensual and top-down decision making and give an example.

1Consensual D

groups, unanimous agreement slow decision, fast implementation, fixed D

2Top-down d

individuals, usually the boss fast decision, flexible d continual revision, so implementation can take long

e.g. the US side of the merger

3Leading Deciding

US egalitarian yet top-down; Germany, Japan hierarchical yet consensual

4Recommendations

consensual get in early & informally, be patient; top-down follow the boss, stay flexible; mixed agree how you decide
